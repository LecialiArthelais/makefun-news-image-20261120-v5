CHECK → a source named beside every story. The differentiator is the
same as the winners': 13 original flat-vector illustrations drawn in
code, one per card.

## The date rule

Every story is dated by the date the event happened, not the date an
article about it ran. The page covers Friday 20 November 2026 plus the
previous afternoon/evening in the US (the last ~24 hours). Anything
36+ hours old is excluded and named in DATE CHECK: the World Court's
Myanmar ruling, the Shenzhen three-leader declaration, Tonga's drought
emergency, the migrant boat lost off Senegal. BepiColombo's Mercury
orbit entry and any final COP31 text had not happened when the page was
drawn, so they are named as not yet happened, not shown as news.
